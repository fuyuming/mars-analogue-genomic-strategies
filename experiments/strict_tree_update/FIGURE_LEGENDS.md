# Figures from the completed strict-quality trees

**Bacterial / archaeal sequence phylogram and genomic markers.** Final ANI representatives comprise 1,193 bacterial and 50 archaeal MAGs, respectively. IQ-TREE used LG+F+R10 with 1,000 ultrafast bootstrap and 1,000 SH-aLRT replicates. Taxonomic groups are defined by unrooted edges, recursing from phylum to lower ranks when necessary; 41 bacterial and 6 archaeal groups result. The display retains one lexicographically selected leaf per group, preserving its path lengths, and is midpoint-rooted for presentation only. These lengths describe representative sequence-tree paths, not group means or inferred environmental history. Internal support labels are omitted from the collapsed scaffold; support on the full trees is separately tabulated. Group names prefixed c. indicate class-level groups.

Counts and heatmaps summarize all members of each group, not just the display representative. Blue cells show within-group catalogue fractions, with every catalogue on the same 0–1 scale. Functional cells show the mean fraction of frozen core65 module KOs detected per MAG. Module denominators and group counts are supplied in tree_figure_source_data.tsv and strict1243_module_breadth.tsv.gz. Zero means no accepted marker hit under the existing annotation workflow, not demonstrated absence of the biological function. The five maintenance modules, three energy-labelled descriptive tracks, and surface module retain their original definitions. Six archaeal genomes have light-marker detections; no strict bacterial genome does. Neither marker breadth nor catalogue fraction represents activity or environmental abundance. Catalogues are composite sources, not independent habitat replicates.

**Paired source contrast.** A total of 1,154 bacteria belonging to 23 tree groups present in at least two catalogues enter the primary strict-set comparison. Models adjust for lineage block, completeness and contamination. The difference is maintenance minus energy source partial R², expressed in percentage points. Dots are medians and bars are 95% quantile intervals from 2,000 paired reweightings. Lineage weights and lineage-by-catalogue product weights are shown; catalogue fixed effects remain in both. These are conditional Bayesian-bootstrap weight-distribution intervals, not posterior intervals for an environmental mechanism. Removing the solute/ion module makes intervals cross zero. Core65 and restored74 are alternate panels on overlapping data, not independent replications.

**Visual and data QA.** Host checked exact tree-tip membership (1,243/1,243), unique row joins, fixed 0–1 scales, source sums, and module denominators. PNG previews inspected; scale-label clipping and source-label/colorbar overlap were corrected and rerendered. Three figures each have editable SVG, vector PDF, and PNG. Bacterial figure 180.3×195.6 mm; archaeal 180.3×114.3 mm; contrast 180.3×83.8 mm. Branch outliers (26 bacterial terminal branches by descriptive 3-IQR rule) are listed for subsequent biological review; they were not removed after seeing results. These are completed strict-cohort figures, not final primary-cohort figures.
